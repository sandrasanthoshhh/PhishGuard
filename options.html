About PhishGuard

PhishGuard analyzes URLs, domains, redirects, credential forms, and webpage content to calculate a phishing risk score.

Risk levels:
LOW — minimal indicators
SUSPICIOUS — some suspicious indicators
HIGH — significant indicators
CRITICAL — severe indicators
